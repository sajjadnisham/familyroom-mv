Combos

A cup and a plate that go together.

Six pairings pulled from the real menu — a drink and a dish that suit each other, at a combined price a little under ordering both apart.

These pairings are a suggestion, not a menu item. Every drink and dish here is real and already on the menu; the combo framing and the discounted total are this page's own proposal, not something the café currently offers. If they like the idea, it is a five-minute change to make it official — a line on the till, a card on the counter. No price on this page is real; see Menu for the same caveat.
