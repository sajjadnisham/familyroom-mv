Order at the counter. Breakfast runs all day, and the kitchen stays on until late.

Real dishes, sample prices. The photographed items come from the café’s own promotional artwork and guest photos; the rest come from public reviews between 2017 and 2026. No price here is real. They are anchored to the one figure we could find — a Nov 2019 review paid MVR 163 for pancakes, avocado toast and two donuts — scaled for 2026 so the page reads plausibly in a demo. The café replaces every number before launch. Dietary labels stay off until confirmed.
