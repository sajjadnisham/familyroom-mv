Our coffee

The café belongs to the roaster.

Family Room is the coffee house of Coffee Lab Maldives, which has been roasting specialty coffee here since 2014. The beans in your cup were roasted by the same people who serve it.

A micro-roastery, not a wholesale bag

Coffee Lab runs its own micro-roastery in the Maldives and supplies cafés and resorts across the country. That means the coffee here is roasted in small batches, close by, and served fresh rather than shipped in.

Source: Corporate Maldives, 2017, and the café’s own retail bags. Confirm current roasting setup

How we brew

Two ways to drink it

  1. Espresso, with fresh milk

    Flat whites, lattes and cappuccinos, pulled by trained baristas. This is what most guests order, and it is what nine years of reviews keep praising.

  2. Hand-brewed filter

    A single origin, brewed to order, for when you want to taste the coffee on its own. Ask what is on the bar today.

The people behind it

Coffee Lab Maldives is led by Ahmed “Afrah” Mohamed, reported to be the first Maldivian Q-Grader and an SCA-authorised trainer. The company announced a Maldives Coffee Academy to train baristas locally.

On the floor, the compliment that turns up in reviews more often than any other is simply the staff: friendly, patient, and remembered by name.

The idea

A living room that happens to be on the beach

The concept has always been a room you can settle into rather than a counter you pass through: a small air-conditioned space inside, seats on the sand outside, books and board games on the shelf, and a corner for children. Guests have been calling it “cozy” and “homely” since 2017.

Check before publishing: the books, board games and kids’ corner are evidenced between 2017 and 2019 only. A Jan 2026 review also mentions a seating time limit and a no-laptop rule at busy times — if those rules are current, the Visit page is where they belong, stated plainly, so nobody is surprised.
