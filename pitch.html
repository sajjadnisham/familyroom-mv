The pitch

The “Website” button already exists. It just goes to the wrong place.

Family Room Coffee has 435 Google reviews at 4.6 and ranks #1 for coffee & tea in Hulhumalé. The demand is already there. What is missing is the page it should land on.

The same result, pointing at a page that answers the three questions people actually have: what is on the menu, what it costs, and whether you are open right now. Domain availability unchecked.

What this demo shows

  • A live “open now” indicator that understands the Friday prayer break and the 01:30 close — see it on the homepage.
  • A tabbed menu that doubles as the in-store QR menu, so there is one thing to update and nothing to reprint — see it.
  • House rules written down on the Visit page, which is what would have prevented the January 2026 one-star review about the seating limit — see it.
  • The coffee story, the one thing no competitor on this beach can copy — see it.
  • A sticky Menu · Directions · Call bar on phones, where nearly all of this traffic is.

What the café needs to supply

The demo is honest about its gaps on purpose: every unverified line is tagged. To turn it into a live site we need the logo and brand colours, the current menu with prices, confirmed hours including Friday and Ramadan, a WhatsApp number, the exact map pin, photos they own the rights to, and their sign-off on the Coffee Lab wording.

Two quick wins to offer alongside the site. Point the Google Business Profile’s website field at the new domain, and start replying to reviews on Google and Tripadvisor — there are no owner replies on Tripadvisor at all today.
