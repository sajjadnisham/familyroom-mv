Good to know

Questions guests actually ask

Each of these answers exists because of a review. Every one is a complaint the café can prevent by writing it down.

Can I work on a laptop?

Answer to be confirmed. A guest in January 2026 was asked to leave after about half an hour and told laptops were not allowed. If that is the rule at busy times, saying so here — plainly, with the times it applies — costs nothing and prevents the one-star review it caused.

Is there Wi-Fi?

To be confirmed. Wi-Fi was praised regularly in reviews up to about 2021 and is still listed as a feature on Tripadvisor.

Do you deliver?

To be confirmed. There is a listing on the Foodies app (restaurant #93), but whether it is active is not visible from outside. Takeaway is listed as available.

Is it good for children?

To be confirmed. A kids’ corner was listed in 2019, and board games are part of the original concept. The hot chocolate has a long record with younger guests.

Do you sell coffee beans to take home?

To be confirmed. Packaged beans were on sale in 2019, and the parent company is a roaster — so this is likely, but not verified.

How do I pay?

To be confirmed. Visa and Mastercard are listed on Tripadvisor. Worth stating whether local transfer apps are accepted, since that is a common question.

Can I book a table?

No bookings are taken as far as we can tell — it is walk-in, order at the counter. confirm
